How to play

Pick a set. Compare the artist, mapper, stars, BPM and length with the daily answer.

The year is when the set became Ranked or Loved. Yellow means one year off.

Arrows point toward the answer. Each set uses its hardest catch difficulty.

After 3 guesses you can see the map stats, after 5 the difficulty name, and after 7 the background.
