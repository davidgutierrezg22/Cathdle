How to Play

Guess the daily osu!catch beatmapset in 10 attempts.

Choose a beatmapset and compare its information with today's answer. The colors and arrows show how close your guess is.

✓ Green means the value is correct.
≈ Yellow means the value is close.
× Red means the value is incorrect.
↕ Arrows show whether the correct value is higher or lower.

Clues

After 4 attempts: CS, AR, OD and HP can be revealed.

After 7 attempts: the difficulty name can be revealed.

After 9 attempts: the map background can be revealed.

Only the highest-rated osu!catch difficulty from each beatmapset is used.
